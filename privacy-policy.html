Welcome to Cooking Recipes ("we", "our", or "us"), provided by MInfosoftware. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our mobile app and website (cookingrecipes.minfosoftware.online).

2. Information We Collect

Guest Mode (100% Private, No Login Required):
You can freely browse, search, and cook recipes, use the kitchen timer, portion scaler, and smart pantry without creating an account. Your favorite recipes and grocery lists are kept locally on your phone storage.

A. Optional Account Information (Google Sign-In):
If you optionally sign in with your Google Account, we receive basic public profile information provided by Google:

  • Your Name (for user personalization)
  • Your Email Address (as your account identifier)
  • Profile Picture URL (to display your avatar)

B. User Content: Recipes you create or dish photos you share in the "Made It!" community feed.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Data: Anonymous diagnostic telemetry (device model, OS version, crash logs) used strictly to troubleshoot and improve app performance.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide step-by-step Gujarati, Kathiyawadi, Punjabi, and Indian recipes.
  • Sync favorite recipes, meal plans, and grocery checklists across devices.
  • Power the Smart Pantry ingredient matcher and hands-free voice cooking assistant.

We never sell, trade, or monetize your personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted industry providers for infrastructure:

  • Google Play Services: Core application infrastructure and distribution.
  • Firebase Authentication & Database: Secure user sign-in and cloud sync.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time:

  • You can clear all local application data in your device settings anytime.
  • Permanent Data Deletion: To delete your cloud account and all uploaded recipes/photos, send an email to minfosoftware@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Cooking Recipes Account". We will permanently erase all your data within 7 business days.

6. Security

All network communications between the Cooking Recipes app and our servers use encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). We do not store sensitive payment details or passwords.
