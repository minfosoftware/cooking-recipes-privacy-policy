## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Guest Mode (No Account Required)
You can use the vast majority of our features completely anonymously without signing up:
- Browse, search, and follow 100+ Gujarati, Kathiyawadi, Punjabi, and Indian recipes.
- Use the hands-free step-by-step Voice Cooking Assistant and Kitchen Timers.
- Use the Smart Pantry Matcher and Dynamic Portion Scaler.
- Save favorite recipes and manage your grocery shopping list (stored strictly on your local device).

### B. Optional Account Data (Google Sign-In)
If you optionally sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive the basic public profile data provided by Google:
- **Name:** To personalize your kitchen profile.
- **Email Address:** Used as your unique account credential.
- **Profile Photo URL:** To display your user avatar in the header.

### C. User-Generated Content
If you upload photos of dishes you cooked or leave reviews in the "Made It!" community showcase, this media is stored on our secure servers to display inside the app's community feed.

### D. Technical & Diagnostic Information
We may collect anonymous crash statistics, operating system version, and device model information via Google Play Services to identify bugs and enhance stability.
